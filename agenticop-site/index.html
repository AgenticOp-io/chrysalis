Why this exists

  • Translators that only read source code still lie when behavior drifts from intent.
  • Agents accelerate exploration; they do not replace a corpus and a replay gate.
  • Production traffic, SQL, and sessions become the contract agents must satisfy.

The tension

Teams want speed from AI-assisted refactors. Compliance and uptime demand evidence. AgenticOp holds both: humans own scope and risk; automation expands surface area; Chrysalis verify scores every promoted route against recorded reality.

Specification from reality

Design docs rot. Captured requests, responses, and queries do not.

No silent wrong output

Unsupported constructs become typed holes with provenance, not pretend-complete code.

Cut over with control

Approach

A typical engagement threads corpus design, CI gates, and staged routing. Exact scope follows your stack; the loop stays the same.

Observe and shape the corpus

Record representative traffic and side effects. Redact what policy requires; keep enough signal for replay to be meaningful.

Ingest and emit with provenance

PHP AST to WebIR, then to your chosen Node stack. Every emitted route carries lineage back to source so drift is attributable.

Verify before you celebrate

Replay the corpus against the modern app; score correctness and chase divergence where semantics matter.

Deploy chimera, then narrow legacy

Route-by-route shadow and canary until cutover confidence clears your bar; session and schema bridges hold dual stacks honest.

FAQ

Is AgenticOp a separate product from Chrysalis?

Chrysalis is the open-source engine. AgenticOp is the practice layer: how we run pilots and operators use the tool chain without skipping verification.

Do agents write production code without review?

No. Agents may draft or refactor; promotion still passes your gates. Replay and holes reporting keep partial output honest.

Where do commercial terms live?

Billing and contracts stay outside the repo. See the commercial playbook in Chrysalis docs/COMMERCIAL.md.

Start from behavior, not hope

Read the design, clone a fixture, and run verify. That loop is the spine of every AgenticOp engagement.
