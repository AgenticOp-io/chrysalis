Start a pilot on staging

Fixed-scope migration programs for legacy PHP, Laravel, WordPress-shaped PHP, and similar stacks. We use production behavior as the contract — not an AI rewrite gamble.

Email hello@agenticop.io LinkedIn

What to send

  • Stack (PHP/Laravel/WordPress/custom) and rough route or module count
  • Whether staging access and oracle capture are possible
  • Target modern stack (Node/Hono, Next, etc.) if known
  • Timeline and who owns cutover risk on your side

Discovery calls are short. We document holes and verify scope explicitly — trace replay is evidence, not a claim of full production parity unless contracted.

Typical offers

Discovery (~1 week)

Route inventory, corpus plan, verify feasibility, written go/no-go.

Pilot (3–4 weeks)

5–15 routes: CWL export, verify report, chimera cutover plan, hole budget.

Run (monthly)

Corpus hygiene, verify in CI, release alignment after a successful pilot.
